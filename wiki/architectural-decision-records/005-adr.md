## ADR-5 — Firestore has exactly one owner

**Status:** Accepted

**Context.** State integrity requires a single authority. If consumers could write
Firestore directly, business rules and validation would be bypassable.

**Decision.** Only `functions/` reads or writes Firestore. Consumers are untrusted
callers; the backend **re-validates every input** at its boundary with Zod and is the
sole authority on what is persisted. The backend is self-contained — `functions/` does
not import `shared/` and calls no third-party API; cross-service knowledge lives on the
consumer side in `shared/`.

**Consequences.**

- Security and consistency rules live in exactly one place.
- The backend can be reasoned about without knowing who called it.
- The client keeps no authoritative state — what it shows is a projection of what the
  backend returns.
